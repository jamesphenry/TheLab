# 2026-10-24 Lesson 1.1 Updated Test

## Question
Does the updated Lesson 1.1 (with mixed coverage) pass the curriculum's assessment criteria?

## Method
Tested with updated exemplars from the curriculum, used best-checkpoint evaluation, 4 seeds, 30 iterations.

## Updated Exemplars
```python
[
    "a b c d e f g",
    "c d e f g h i",
    "m n o p q r s",
    "t u v w x y z",
    "e f g h i j k",
    "p q r s t u",
    "q r s t u v",
    "r s t u v w",
    "o p q r s t",
]
```

## Assessment
Held-out items: ('g h i ', 'j'), ('n o p ', 'q'), ('s t u ', 'v')
Pass criteria: delta_loss > 2.0 AND held accuracy >= 50%

## Results
| Seed | Delta Loss | Held Acc | Passed |
| :-- | :----------- | :--------- | :------- |
| 42  | +6.3990     | 66.67%     | True     |
| 123 | +6.1283     | 0.00%      | False    |
| 7   | +5.2798     | 0.00%      | False    |
| 999 | +7.1229     | 0.00%      | False    |

Mean: Delta +6.23, Held Acc 16.67%

## Findings
- High variance across seeds persists even with improved coverage
- Seed 42 passes well, others fail completely on held-out generation
- Loss delta is always high (>5) but generation accuracy is poor for most seeds
- Best-checkpoint helps but isn't enough to stabilize across all seeds
- The mixed coverage helped in our earlier test setup but results depend heavily on seed/initialization

## Next Steps
- Need to understand why some seeds work well and others don't
- May need different initialization strategies or more robust training
- Or adjust pass criteria to account for seed variance
- Or test with more seeds to understand distribution
