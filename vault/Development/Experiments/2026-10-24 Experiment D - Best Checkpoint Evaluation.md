# 2026-10-24 Experiment D - Best Checkpoint Evaluation

## Question
How important is checkpoint selection for evaluation?

## Hypothesis
Best checkpoint will show better performance than final checkpoint.

## Variables and Conditions
- **Independent variable(s):** Checkpoint selection strategy
- **Measured outcome(s):** Performance difference between best and final
- **Controls:** All formats tested with both strategies

## Results
- Original: Best 25% vs Final 20.83% (gap -4.17%)
- Full Coverage: Best 18.75% vs Final 12.50% (gap -6.25%)
- Mixed Fixed: Best 41.67% vs Final 37.50% (gap -4.17%)

All show best checkpoint better than final. Mixed has smallest relative degradation.

## Conclusion
Checkpoint selection is important. Curriculum should use best-checkpoint evaluation protocol.
