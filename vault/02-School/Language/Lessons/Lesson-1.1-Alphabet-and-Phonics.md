# Lesson 1.1 — Alphabet and Vowel-Consonant Patterns

## Objective
Introduce foundational vowels (A, E, I, O, U), common consonants, and whitespace-delimited word tokens.

## Teaching Material & Exemplars
1. **Alphabet Sequences:**
   - `a b c d e f g h i j k l m n o p q r s t u v w x y z`
2. **Vowel Isolation:**
   - `The vowels are a, e, i, o, u.`
3. **Consonant-Vowel-Consonant (CVC) Words:**
   - `cat, bat, rat, mat, hat`
   - `pen, hen, ten, red, bed`
   - `dog, log, fog, hot, pot`
   - `sun, run, fun, cup, bus`
4. **Sight Words & Basic Associations:**
   - `The cat is red.`
   - `The dog can run.`
   - `A cat sat on a mat.`
   - `The sun is hot.`

## Practice Exercises
- Complete the sequence: `a b c d ` -> `e`
- Word completion: `c a ` -> `t`
- Word association: `The cat can ` -> `run`
