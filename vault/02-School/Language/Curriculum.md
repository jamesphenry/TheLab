# Early Language & Reading Curriculum

## Module 1: Phonics, Letters, and Word Formation
- **Lesson 1.1:** Alphabet & Vowel-Consonant Patterns (A, E, I, O, U)
- **Lesson 1.2:** Whitespace & Word Delimiters (separating tokens into discrete words)
- **Lesson 1.3:** Sight Words (the, a, is, in, it, you, that, he, was, for)

## Module 2: Naming & World Associations
- **Lesson 2.1:** Animals & Sounds ("The cow says moo. The cat says meow.")
- **Lesson 2.2:** Colors & Attributes ("The apple is red. The grass is green.")
- **Lesson 2.3:** Action Verbs (run, jump, play, see, eat)

## Module 3: Sentence Construction & Dialogue
- **Lesson 3.1:** Subject-Verb-Object Structure ("The boy has a toy.")
- **Lesson 3.2:** Simple Question and Response Patterns:
  - Teacher: "What is your name?"
  - Student: "My name is Learner."
- **Lesson 3.3:** Yes / No Inquiries ("Is the sky blue? Yes.")

## Module 4: 1st Grade Story Reading & Retention
- **Lesson 4.1:** Short 3-Sentence Stories ("Sam has a ball. Sam threw the ball. The dog caught it.")
- **Lesson 4.2:** Comprehension Checks ("Who threw the ball?")
