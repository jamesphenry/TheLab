# Lesson 3.2 — Object Completion

## Objective
Teach the model to complete sentences with object nouns in prepositional contexts.

## Teaching Material & Exemplars
1. **On/at/in patterns:**
   - `The cat sat on the ` -> `mat.`
   - `The dog lay on the ` -> `bed.`
   - `The bird sat in the ` -> `nest.`
   - `The frog sat on a ` -> `log.`
   - `The bug sat on the ` -> `leaf.`

2. **Different prepositions:**
   - `The fish swam in the ` -> `pond.`
   - `The pig slept in the ` -> `pen.`
   - `The cow lay in the ` -> `barn.`
   - `The hen sat in the ` -> `coop.`
   - `The rat hid in the ` -> `hole.`

3. **Varied subjects:**
   - `A cat sat on a ` -> `mat.`
   - `The dog ran to the ` -> `park.`
   - `The boy sat on the ` -> `chair.`
   - `The girl sat in the ` -> `box.`
   - `The pup lay on the ` -> `rug.`

## Practice Exercises
- Complete: `The ant sat on a ` -> `rock.`
- Complete: `The duck swam in the ` -> `pond.`
- Complete: `The kid sat on the ` -> `bed.`

## Notes
Prepositional phrases provide strong contextual cues. Keep objects as common concrete nouns. The pattern `on the `, `in the ` creates predictable structure for the model to learn.
