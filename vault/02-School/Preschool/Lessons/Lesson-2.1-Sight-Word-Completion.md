# Lesson 2.1 — Sight Word Completion

## Objective
Teach the model to complete common sentence patterns with appropriate sight words, using pattern completion rather than rule instruction.

## Teaching Material & Exemplars
1. **Basic articles and adjectives:**
   - `The cat is ` -> `big.`
   - `The dog is ` -> `fast.`
   - `The bird is ` -> `small.`
   - `The pig is ` -> `fat.`
   - `The rat is ` -> `wet.`

2. **With varied subjects:**
   - `A cat is ` -> `soft.`
   - `A bug is ` -> `tiny.`
   - `The fox is ` -> `quick.`
   - `The ant is ` -> `slow.`
   - `The hen is ` -> `red.`

3. **Simple action states:**
   - `The boy is ` -> `sad.`
   - `The girl is ` -> `mad.`
   - `The man is ` -> `tall.`
   - `The kid is ` -> `glad.`
   - `The pup is ` -> `wet.`

## Practice Exercises
- Complete: `The rat is ` -> `bad.`
- Complete: `A bat is ` -> `black.`
- Complete: `The hog is ` -> `big.`

## Notes
Keep adjectives short (3-5 letters) and use high-frequency patterns. The model learns from the statistical association between sentence context and completions. Vary subjects to prevent memorization of specific subject-verb pairs.
