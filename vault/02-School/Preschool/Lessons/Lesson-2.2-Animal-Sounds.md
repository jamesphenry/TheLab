# Lesson 2.2 — Animal Sounds

## Objective
Teach the model to complete animal sound patterns in sentence contexts.

## Teaching Material & Exemplars
1. **Common farm animals:**
   - `The cow says ` -> `moo.`
   - `The pig says ` -> `oink.`
   - `The duck says ` -> `quack.`
   - `The hen says ` -> `cluck.`
   - `The sheep says ` -> `baa.`

2. **Other animals:**
   - `The dog says ` -> `woof.`
   - `The cat says ` -> `meow.`
   - `The frog says ` -> `ribbit.`
   - `The rooster says ` -> `cock.`
   - `The horse says ` -> `neigh.`

3. **Varied contexts:**
   - `A cow says ` -> `moo.`
   - `The dog says ` -> `bark.`
   - `A duck says ` -> `quack.`
   - `The chick says ` -> `peep.`
   - `The goat says ` -> `baa.`

## Practice Exercises
- Complete: `The frog says ` -> `ribbit.`
- Complete: `The duck says ` -> `quack.`
- Complete: `The pig says ` -> `oink.`

## Notes
Animal sounds use onomatopoeia which are often short, repetitive strings. The model learns these as fixed completions in this specific pattern. Keep contexts consistent (`says `) to make the pattern clear.
