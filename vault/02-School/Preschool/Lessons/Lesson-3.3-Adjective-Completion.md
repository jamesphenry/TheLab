# Lesson 3.3 — Adjective Completion

## Objective
Teach the model to fill in descriptive adjectives in simple sentence patterns.

## Teaching Material & Exemplars
1. **Size and temperature:**
   - `The sun is ` -> `hot.`
   - `The ice is ` -> `cold.`
   - `The ant is ` -> `small.`
   - `The bear is ` -> `big.`
   - `The bug is ` -> `tiny.`

2. **Physical properties:**
   - `The rock is ` -> `hard.`
   - `The mud is ` -> `soft.`
   - `The cake is ` -> `sweet.`
   - `The lemon is ` -> `sour.`
   - `The cloth is ` -> `wet.`

3. **Describing animals/objects:**
   - `The cat is ` -> `soft.`
   - `The dog is ` -> `wet.`
   - `The ball is ` -> `round.`
   - `The box is ` -> `square.`
   - `The cup is ` -> `full.`

## Practice Exercises
- Complete: `The snow is ` -> `cold.`
- Complete: `The pillow is ` -> `soft.`
- Complete: `The nut is ` -> `hard.`

## Notes
Adjectives can appear in different positions. Here we focus on the predicate position (`is + adjective`) which gives a clean, consistent pattern. Keep adjectives short and concrete.
