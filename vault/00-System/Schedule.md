# Schedule & Temporal Routines

## Routine Phases
1. **Morning Review:** Learner inspects `01-Learner/Goals.md`, recent test feedback, and unresolved questions.
2. **Instruction Period:** Teacher presents concept from active curriculum in `02-School/<Subject>/Lessons/`.
3. **Lab / Practice Period:** Learner works on exercises independently with access to sandboxed tooling.
4. **Examination & Assessment:** Learner answers unfamiliar assessment tasks. Automated/teacher rubric evaluation logged in `07-Reports/`.
5. **Decision Step:** Learner evaluates performance vs self-assessment and chooses next activity (Remediation, Practice, Advance, or Consultation).
6. **Reflection / Dream Cycle:** Idle period during which learner summarizes lessons, extracts principles, or explores hypotheses.
7. **Human Guidance / Social Exchange:** Casual conversation with human guide.
