Reusable note templates for this vault. Copy the closest template into its destination folder, replace placeholders, and follow [[00-System/Documentation Standard|Documentation Standard]].

## Templates

- [[Experiment Template]] — concise scientific-method record for a question, evidence, hypothesis, method, procedure, results, interpretation, conclusion, provenance, and possible follow-up ideas.
- [[Research Audit Template]] — synthesis across experiments, theories, and source records.
- [[Decision Template]] — architecture or curriculum decisions (ADR).
- [[Plan Template]] — scoped work plans.
- [[Log Template]] and [[Development/Logs/Session Log Template|Session Log Template]] — general and dated session records.
- [[Debugging Template]] — reproducible failures and resolution records.

Templates are starting structures, not evidence. Omit irrelevant sections, but retain the distinctions between hypothesis, method, observation, interpretation, limitations, and conclusion where they apply.
